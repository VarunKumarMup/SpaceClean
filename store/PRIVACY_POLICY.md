# Privacy Policy — SpaceClean

**Effective date:** 29 June 2026
**App:** SpaceClean (`io.github.varunkumar.spaceclean`)
**Developer contact:** varunkumarmuppuri@gmail.com

## Summary

SpaceClean is a **100% offline** storage cleaner and file manager. It **does not collect,
transmit, sell, or share any personal data**. Everything the app does happens **entirely on
your device**. The app contains **no internet permission**, no advertising, no analytics, and
no third‑party SDKs — so there is no technical means by which your data could leave your phone.

## Data we collect

**None.** SpaceClean does not collect or transmit any personal or device information. It has no
servers, no accounts, and no network code.

The app reads your files, media, and the list of installed apps **only on your device and only
to show them to you and let you delete or manage them**. This information is never copied off the
device and is not retained by the developer in any form.

## Permissions and why they are used

| Permission | Why the app needs it |
|---|---|
| **All files access** (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) | To scan, preview, and delete files across your storage (photos, videos, documents, downloads, duplicates, leftover folders). Required for a file‑manager/cleaner on Android 11+. |
| **Read media** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`/`VIDEO`/`AUDIO`) | Granular access to your media on Android 13+ for the photo/video/audio managers. |
| **Read external storage** (Android ≤10 only) | Legacy storage access on older devices. |
| **Query all packages** (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`) | To list your installed apps and their sizes in the App Storage manager. The app list is shown only to you and never leaves the device. |
| **Request delete packages** (`REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES`) | To open Android's standard uninstall dialog when you choose to remove an app. |

The app intentionally **does not request the INTERNET permission.**

## How your data is used

All scanning, previewing, hashing (for duplicate detection), and deletion happens locally on
your device. Deleted media is moved to your device's system Trash where supported (recoverable
for about 30 days via your Files/Photos app); some items, such as chat‑app media and folders you
select manually, are deleted directly.

## Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because no data is collected or leaves your device.

## Children's privacy

SpaceClean does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

## Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no off‑device data to secure. Your files
remain under your control on your device at all times.
